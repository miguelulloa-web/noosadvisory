# Task Packet — TP-08: Execution of NoosAdvisory Visual Design System v1.0 (Executive Intelligence / Modern Clean)

## Objective
Implement the official **NoosAdvisory Visual Design System v1.0** across the Landing Page, transitioning from the dark-mode / neon aesthetic to a **Modern Clean, Executive Intelligence** design direction (predominantly light, spacious, corporate, evidence-driven, single typography Inter, 70/20/7/3 chromatic distribution), while preserving 100% of validated business copy and ensuring WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility and SEO + AI Discoverability.

## Verifiable Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **CRIT-01 (Color Palette & Chromatic Distribution):** Implements official palette tokens: 70% Neutral/White (`#FFFFFF`, `#F7F9FC`), 20% Midnight Navy (`#0B1930`), 7% Corporate Blue (`#185ADB`), 3% Intelligent Teal (`#00A896`), Graphite (`#344054`), Slate (`#667085`), Border (`#DDE3EA`). Cero multicolor dark neon gradients.
- [ ] **CRIT-02 (Typography & Scale):** Single font family `Inter` used across entire site with strict optical scale: Display/Hero (56–64px), H1 (48–56px), H2 (36–44px), H3 (24–30px), Body (16–18px), Small/Metadata (13–14px).
- [ ] **CRIT-03 (Whitespace, Layout & Soft Elevation):** Generous vertical whitespace (100–120px section padding), alternating White / Soft Background `#F7F9FC` sections, subtle borders (`#DDE3EA`), and Soft Elevation shadows (`0 2px 4px rgba(11,25,48,0.04), 0 12px 24px rgba(11,25,48,0.06)`).
- [ ] **CRIT-04 (Executive Hero & Value Metrics):** Clean light hero in Midnight Navy, Graphite lead text, Corporate Blue primary button, secondary button with discrete border, and clean metrics card.
- [ ] **CRIT-05 (AAGm Product-First Case & Portafolio):** Presentation of Project 01 following `Challenge → Approach → Solution → AI → Outcome` and the 6-step flow `Entender → Definir → Diseñar → Construir → Validar → Evolucionar`, with clean visual architecture and live AAGm demonstration banner.
- [ ] **CRIT-06 (Iconography & Systems Graphic Language):** Consistent linear geometric icons in Midnight Navy, Corporate Blue, and Intelligent Teal. Cero sci-fi cliches (no humanoid robots or glowing circuits).
- [ ] **CRIT-07 (Purposeful Motion & Microinteractions):** Discreet, functional transitions (150–350ms) for hover, buttons, and tab transitions without layout shifts.
- [ ] **CRIT-08 (WCAG 2.2 AA Accessibility):** Color contrast ratios >= 4.5:1 across all text, keyboard navigation support, visible focus rings, and valid ARIA attributes.
- [ ] **CRIT-09 (Responsive Desktop, Tablet & Mobile):** Flawless presentation and touch-friendly controls across Desktop (1440px / 1200px), Tablet (768px - 1024px), and Smartphone (375px - 414px) with accessible navigation drawer.
- [ ] **CRIT-10 (SEO & AI Discoverability):** Strict HTML5 semantic hierarchy (`header`, `nav`, `main`, `section`, `article`, `footer`, single `h1`), Open Graph tags, and Schema.org JSON-LD structured data.
